Chapter 1 · the week most designers know

You design it. Someone else decides if it worked.

MondayA brief lands. The problem is already decided for you.

Chapter 8 · measure · data argues with taste

The idea was a "most read" section. The data said no.

What we felt

Show the popular articles. Social proof works.

What we found

Too few readers to call anything popular, and the "top" article changed depending on how you counted.

What we designed

Three hand-picked articles, one per service. No fake popularity.

And in the sign-up flow: two real people stopped at question five and nobody followed up. The fix was a daily call list, not a new screen.
